enum class response
strong

Response codes returned by event callbacks.

Controls whether a callback continues receiving events or is automatically removed.

Enumerator
ok 

Continue receiving events.

The callback remains registered and will be invoked on future events.

unsubscribe 

Unsubscribe after this callback.

The callback is automatically removed after returning and will not receive future events. Useful for one-shot callbacks.
